# Evidence notes

## What the prototype can say

- SCN2A-related disorders span gain-, loss-, and mixed-function variants, and functional effect is associated with different phenotype patterns.
- Gain-of-function SCN2A, SCN3A, and SCN8A variants have published cross-gene mechanistic and early-onset epilepsy overlap.
- The International SCN8A Patient Registry is a longitudinal research resource.
- The DRAGONFLY Study is a global SCN2A patient registry.
- A research-match ranking can be used to prioritize which communities or assets deserve expert review.

## What the prototype must not say

- That two diseases are clinically equivalent.
- That a registry or endpoint can be reused without expert validation.
- That a treatment effective in one sodium-channel disorder should be used in another.
- That the Research Match Score is a medical probability.
- That the system provides medical advice.

## Evidence semantics

- **Observed:** directly represented by a source, e.g. a registry exists.
- **Supported association:** evidence supports a relationship but it is not deterministic.
- **Cross-disease inference:** Beacon combines sourced relationships to produce a research lead that requires expert review.
